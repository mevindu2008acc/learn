About Learno

Knowledge at your fingertips — a free educational platform built with ❤️ for Sri Lankan A/L Commerce students.

📖 Our Story

Learno was created with a simple goal — to make quality A/L study materials freely accessible to every Commerce stream student in Sri Lanka.

As a fellow student myself, I understand the struggle of searching for past papers, short notes, and lesson-wise resources scattered across the internet. Learno brings all of them together in one clean, easy-to-use platform.

Whether you're preparing for Accounting, Business Studies, Economics, or ICT — Learno is here to support you on your journey to A/L success. 🎓
